Chronicle is your personal journal, inspired by the power and simplicity of Evernote, but designed to be unique and secure.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **Fast & Lightweight:** Instant startup with zero bloatware.
- 🎨 **Minimalist Design:** Clean, intuitive interface optimized for focus.
- ✏️ **Effortless Note Management:** Quickly create, edit, organize, and delete notes.
- 🌍 **Multilingual Support:** Fully translated into 12 major languages.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First:** Your notes and journal entries stay safely on your device.
- 🛠️ **100% Open Source:** Fully transparent and open to community contributions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **IDE:** Android Studio
- **Minimum SDK:** Android 8.0 (API level 26) or higher
- **UI Framework:** Android Jetpack Components
- **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
